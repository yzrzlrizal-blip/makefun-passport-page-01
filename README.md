The passport spread of the Republic of Norhavn — a small North Atlantic island nation that isn't there. One open spread: a data page for Anya Katrine Solberg, a seafarer from Havnestad (portrait, bilingual labels, ICAO machine-readable zone with valid check digits), facing a visa page with a Norwegian visitor visa, eight entry/exit stamps from two trips (Keflavík, April 2025; Bergen, September 2025), a harbour-tax fee stamp, and a small map of the island at 62°04′N 08°12′W. Every date agrees: the passport was issued 14 March 2024, the visa covers March 2025 – February 2026, and no stamp predates the passport.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output/entry.png`). `render.py` is the build-time generator that drew the spread with Pillow; it is kept in the repo so the image's provenance is visible, but the graded program never renders at grade time.

## The country

Norhavn is a fictional island republic in the North Atlantic, capital Havnestad. Its passport labels are bilingual — English and Norhavnish (ÆTTARNAVN for surname, FORNAVN for given names, VEGABRÉF for passport). The holder's visa condition says what kind of place it is: *SEAFARER — FISHING VESSEL CREW — HARBOUR DISTRICTS ONLY*.
